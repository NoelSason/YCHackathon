# Demo script (draft)

Owner: **Noel**. Three minutes. Every `N` gets filled from the dashboard on Sunday morning. Never
type a number that the logs didn't produce. Rehearse twice between 10:00 and 11:00 AM.

## Before going on

- [ ] Every demo check green (`docs/master-plan.md`, "Demo readiness")
- [ ] Dashboard open on the main screen, live phone view showing
- [ ] QR code slide ready (the game, plus the "free levels for your game" form)
- [ ] Backup recording on a second laptop, labeled as a recording
- [ ] One real customer thread or payment ready to click, if we have one

## The three minutes

**0:00–0:20: the result.** Open on the dashboard.

> "At 2 AM we stopped touching this company. Since then its agents designed N levels across three
> games, tested each one hundreds of times, shipped N packs, retuned N levels from how N players did,
> pitched N studios, and spent $N. We stepped in N times, and here's each one."

**0:20–1:10: one level's life.** Click one level in the activity log.

> "The Designer wrote it. The solver said 3% of players would win, too hard. The Tuner got it to
> 41%. A real phone played it. Then real players won N% of the time, and the CEO adjusted it."

**1:10–1:40: one decision we didn't make.** Open the CEO's decision record.

> "Nobody told it to do this. It saw [what it saw], chose [what it chose], and expected [expected].
> Here's what actually happened."

**1:40–2:20: the live part.** QR code on screen.

> "Scan this and play a level our agents made a minute ago." The Mobilerun iPhone plays it on the
> big screen at the same time.

**2:20–3:00: the business.** Open the pipeline.

> "The agents read N reviews across N puzzle games and found studios whose players are telling
> them what's wrong. Parking Loop, for example: 7 of its 86 newest reviews say it's too easy. The
> agents made sample levels for each studio before writing to them. N studios emailed, N replied,
> $N collected." Then the bottom-up math, with a source for every number.

> "If we leave it running, tomorrow it will have shipped more levels, learned from more players,
> and pitched more studios. We set the goal and the budget."

## If something breaks on stage

- Phone view frozen: switch to the recording of the same playtest, and say it's a recording.
- Dashboard down: open the activity log in Neon directly.
- Never claim a step that didn't happen.
