# Demo video script

The narration for the 1:59 demo video (`LevelAI_demo_new_voice.mp4`), split into three parts.
Times are where each line starts in that video. The bracketed note says what's on screen.

"Naive" and "Mobile Run" are spelled that way only so the voice says them correctly. In anything
written, use **Naïve** and **Mobilerun**.

## Part 1: The problem, and a company that runs itself (0:00–0:30)

**[0:00, home page]**
Puzzle games live or die by their levels. Players finish them in minutes, and studios spend months
making more. Level AI is a level studio that runs itself.

**[0:13, live agent console]**
This is our live console. Twenty-one agents and programs run the studio, with the agents built on
the Naive SDK. They've taken over two thousand actions, every one logged in Neon, and this clock
shows how long it has run since a person last stepped in.

## Part 2: What the agents do (0:30–1:01)

**[0:30, research card, then the outreach board]**
Our research agent reads app store reviews, looking for players who say a game's levels are too
hard, too easy, or running out. It has read over one hundred and nine thousand. When enough players
agree, our sales agent emails the studio and quotes its own players. Twenty-one studios so far, and
no replies yet.

**[0:52, the real iPhone]**
Before levels ship, our playtest agent plays them on a real cloud iPhone through Mobile Run, and
writes a design note about what it saw.

## Part 3: The product, the money, and the honesty (1:01–1:59)

**[1:01, games hub, then Splash Sort solved]**
And here is the product: levels you can play right now. Our Designer agent and a level compiler
generate them. Then bots and exact solvers play every level again and again, and we tune it until
its win rate lands on the target.

**[1:19, Turbo Lanes, Lot Escape, Mosslight Trails]**
That's almost three hundred thousand bot games so far. Nearly twelve hundred verified levels are
live across five games, and every time players finish fifty, the system builds fifty more.

**[1:32, pricing, then $0 earned]**
Studios can order a phone playtest or a level pack, and checkout is live. Nobody has paid us yet,
so we've earned zero dollars, and we say so.

**[1:43, human step-ins, then the closing card]**
Finally, here is every time a person stepped in, with the reason. Twenty-one times, all logged. We
set the goal and the budget. The agents run the studio. This is Level AI.
