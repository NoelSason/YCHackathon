# Demo video script

**Video:** https://drive.google.com/file/d/1F3cyK9m_jIign3evCkafnawHXRQf-ZvV/view?usp=sharing

The video is 1:59, 1080p, with no voiceover. Read this script over it, live or recorded. Each line
starts at the time shown. The bracketed note says what's on screen.

Every part was timed read aloud at a normal pace and fits its scene.

**[0:00, home page]**
Puzzle games live on levels. Players finish them in minutes. Studios take months to make more.
Level AI is a level studio run by agents.

**[0:10, live console]**
21 agents and programs run this company. The agents use the Naïve SDK. Over 2,000 actions, all
logged in Neon. The clock shows time since a person last stepped in.

**[0:24, research agent]**
The research agent reads app store reviews through Monid. It looks for levels that are too hard,
too easy, or running out. Over 109,000 read.

**[0:34, outreach board]**
When enough players agree, the sales agent emails the studio and quotes its own players. 21
studios so far.

**[0:42, emails clip]**
It pitches agencies too. Our team played this agency. It replied in 23 seconds.

**[0:49, iPhone playtest]**
Before a level ships, the playtest agent plays it on a real cloud iPhone through Mobilerun. Then
it writes a design note.

**[0:58, X profile]**
The marketing agent posts our research on X, at @uselevelai, from that same phone.

**[1:04, games hub, then Splash Sort]**
This is the product: levels you can play right now. The Designer agent and our level compiler
make them. Bots and exact solvers play each level again and again, and the tuner adjusts it until
the win rate hits the target.

**[1:19, Turbo Lanes, Lot Escape, Mosslight Trails]**
Close to 300,000 bot games so far. About 1,200 verified levels are live across five games. When
players finish 50, the system builds 50 more.

**[1:32, pricing]**
Studios can order a playtest, a level pack, or a monthly plan. Checkout is live.

**[1:37, developer API clip]**
Or they plug in their own level builder. Our agents test and tune it. This test pack took 14
seconds.

**[1:45, human step-ins, then the end card]**
Every time a person stepped in, it's logged here with the reason. We set the goal and the budget.
The agents run the studio. This is Level AI.

## Notes

- Say "@uselevelai" as "use Level AI".
- The numbers are what the screens showed at 8:48 AM PT on Nov 8. The script doesn't say the
  step-in count because it may have gone up since then, and an old, lower number would understate
  how often people stepped in.
- Keep "Our team played this agency" in any rewording. The clips label that agency as a test run by
  our team.
