Goal

Swipe a tile toward a neighbor, or tap two neighbors, to swap them. A swap counts only if it lines up three or more of a kind; otherwise the tiles slide back and you keep the move. Match four in a line for a tile that clears its row or column, an L or T shape for one that clears the tiles around it, and five in a line for one that clears every tile of a color. Ice breaks when you clear a match on top of it. If no swap is left, the board shuffles itself.
