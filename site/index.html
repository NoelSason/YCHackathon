How it works

You tell us how hard your game should feel. We make levels that feel that way, and we prove it before you ship.

  1. 1

    We listen to your players

    We read what your players say about difficulty: where they get stuck, where they get bored, and when they run out of levels.

  2. 2

    We design new levels

    Fresh levels in your game's style, aimed at the difficulty curve you choose.

  3. 3

    We play every level

    Each level is played hundreds of times and tuned until it hits its target, then checked on real phones.

  4. 4

    You ship

    You get ready-to-use levels, with how hard each one is and how players are likely to do.

How we measure difficulty

Difficulty is a number we measure, not a guess. Here is how we get it.

  1. Three kinds of simulated players

    Each level is played hundreds of times by three simulated players: one that moves at random, a greedy one that takes the best-looking move, and one that looks a few moves ahead.

  2. A score you can read

    A level’s difficulty is the share of games those players win, plus how many moves they had left when they won.

  3. Tuned to your target

    We adjust the moves allowed, the blockers, and the layout until the level lands within 5 points of your target win rate. Levels that never get there are thrown out.

  4. Checked for a solution

    For search puzzles, we also confirm each level can be solved and find its fewest moves.

  5. Played on a real iPhone

    Last, a real iPhone plays the level to catch loading, touch, and layout problems.

The honest limit

Simulated players aren’t people. So for your game, we compare our predictions with your real completion and quit rates, then recalibrate. That’s also how you can check us: ask for a difficulty audit below.
