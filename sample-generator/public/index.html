A sample level generator

This endpoint stands in for a game studio's own level generator. Level AI built it to show how a studio connects its game: it makes parking-puzzle levels on request and plays them with its own test player, and Level AI's agents tune each level to a target difficulty.

  • POST /generate makes a level for a seed and a difficulty from 0 to 1.
  • POST /evaluate plays a level many times with a casual test player (best move most of the time, a random move 30% of the time) and reports the wins.
  • POST /validate checks a level.

Every request must be signed by Level AI. Unsigned requests are refused.

How to build your own: Bring your own generator.
